How "2026 Realizable" is calculated
Replaces a flat probability-weighted number with a time-boxed one — what actually lands as revenue inside calendar 2026

For each open opportunity: (Y1 Expected Revenue ÷ 12) × Probability × Pro-ration months, where Pro-ration months is the time between (Close Date + 60-day ramp) and 12/31/2026, capped between 0 and 12. A deal doesn't generate revenue the moment it closes — this bakes in a 2-month onboarding/integration ramp before the monthly run-rate starts, and only counts the months that actually fall within this calendar year. A deal closing in Q4 2026 (or one that's already overdue with a distant close date) can carry real Y1 value but contribute little-to-nothing to 2026 — this metric surfaces that instead of hiding it inside a flat weighted average.
